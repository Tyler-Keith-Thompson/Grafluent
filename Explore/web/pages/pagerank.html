Which pages on a web matter most?

The ideaA bored random surfer

  
Imagine someone browsing at random: they follow a link, and every so often they get bored and jump to any page at all. Over a long session, the share of time they spend on each page is its rank. A link from an important page is worth more than one from an obscure page. The damping factor sets how often they keep clicking instead of jumping, usually 85%.

  
Grafluent computes it by power iteration: start everyone equal, pass each page’s rank along its links, and repeat until nothing moves by more than the tolerance. A page with no links out shares its rank with every page.


  
Where it breaksPages that hoard rank

  
A tight cluster of pages that only link to each other can soak up rank. The random jump keeps that in check, and turning the damping all the way up shows what happens without it: the surfer can get stuck going round, and the scores may never settle.
