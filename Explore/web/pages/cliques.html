Cliques

Say itBRON KER-boss/ˈbrɔn ˈkɛrbɔs/

Which groups of people have all met each other?

What it’s forGroups where everyone knows everyone

A clique is a group in which every pair is linked. It is maximal when nobody else could join it. It is maximum when no clique anywhere is bigger. Bron–Kerbosch lists every maximal clique.

Reach for it to find tight groups in a social network, proteins that all bind each other, or sets of tasks that all conflict. It is fast on sparse networks, where each vertex has few links.

Compared withOther ways to find tight groups

Cliques are the strictest kind of group, and listing them can take exponential time. When a looser group will do, cores and communities are much faster.

Example: eight guests at a party, with a line between each pair who have met.

The ideaGrow a group, remember who could join

Bron–Kerbosch keeps three sets. R is the group being grown. P holds the guests who could still join it, because they have met every member. X holds guests who could join too but have already been tried. Their groups have been found already.

Each call adds one guest from P to R. It keeps only that guest’s friends in P and X, and calls itself again. When P and X are both empty, nobody can join and nobody has been missed. Then R is a maximal clique. When the call returns, the guest moves from P to X.

The pivot saves most of the work. This rule is Tomita’s. Pick the guest in P or X who knows the most of P. Any group made only of the pivot’s friends could add the pivot too. So it will turn up in the call that adds the pivot. Only the guests the pivot hasn’t met need calls of their own. Grafluent also starts the outer calls in degeneracy order, which keeps P small on sparse networks.

Where it breaksThere can be very many cliques

A graph can have about 3n/3 maximal cliques, and listing them takes at least that long. The search is fast on sparse real networks, such as who has met whom. It is hopeless on large dense ones. To find only the largest, maximumClique() prunes with bounds instead of listing everything.
