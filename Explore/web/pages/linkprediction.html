Friend suggestions

Say itah-DAH-mitch/aˈdaːmit͡ʃ/

Ana has three friends. Of the people she hasn’t met, who is she most likely to get on with?

Example: nine people and a line between each pair of friends. Ben knows almost everyone. Dee knows only two people.

What it’s forGuessing the next link

Two people with a friend in common are likely to become friends. Guessing which missing links will appear is called link prediction. The simplest scores look only at the friends two people share.

Reach for these scores to suggest people you may know, to recommend a paper that cites the same work, or to spot a link a dataset is missing. They are cheap, so you can score every pair that is two steps apart.

Compared withOther ways to score a pair

All of these are local, so they score a pair in the time it takes to read two lists of friends. None of them can suggest someone three steps away.

The ideaFriends of friends, weighed

The Jaccard coefficient divides the friends two people share by all the friends either one has. It lies between 0 and 1. It doesn’t favour people who simply know a lot of others.

The Adamic–Adar index adds up the shared friends, but weighs each one by 1 / ln(its number of friends). A shared friend who knows only two people is a strong hint. One who knows everybody is a weak hint, because they connect almost everyone. So Adamic–Adar ranks Ivy, met through Cy and Dee, above Eli, met through Cy and the very popular Ben. Jaccard calls them even.

Each score looks only at the two people’s friends. So jaccardCoefficients(of:) and adamicAdarIndices(of:) score many pairs cheaply.

Where it breaksOnly two steps away

Both scores are 0 for anyone without a friend in common. So they never suggest someone three steps away, and a newcomer with no friends gets no suggestions at all. Scores that count longer paths, such as the Katz index or random walks with restart, reach further at more cost. Real recommenders also use what the network doesn’t show, such as where people live, work or went to school, and which suggestions were accepted before.
